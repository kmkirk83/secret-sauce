# Secret Sauce

**Proven monetization playbook used by high-earning indie developers and solo SaaS founders (2025–2026 patterns).**

This is a Grok skill that encodes the repeatable practices of the most profitable solo and small-team developers. It prioritizes real revenue signals over theoretical product excellence.

## What it does

- Generates product ideas with clear paths to $5k–$50k+ MRR
- Applies strict filters against thin AI wrappers and “AI for everyone” concepts
- Provides niche monetization tactics, pricing patterns, and acquisition channels that actually convert
- Includes a ready-to-use structured prompt for idea generation
- References public high-performing indie examples

## Installation (Grok / Agents)

1. Copy the `secret-sauce` folder into your skills directory (usually `~/.grok/skills/` or the equivalent in your agent environment).
2. Ensure `SKILL.md` is present at the root of the folder.
3. Restart or reload skills if required by your runtime.

The skill will activate on triggers such as:
- “use secret sauce”
- “generate ideas with secret sauce”
- “monetization tactics”
- “indie revenue / micro-SaaS”
- “profitable solo / indie teams”

## Core Principles (summary)

1. Extreme niche + real pain + proven willingness to pay
2. Charge from day one
3. Portfolio + high shipping cadence
4. Distribution is the primary moat
5. Workflow over feature or prompt
6. High margins through automation and pricing power
7. Structured prompts as leverage, not the product
